Above is some art I had to do for my image editing section during Year 10 Digitech. The first picture you see is a pixel of a snowman. I used a programme called Aseprite to draw in the pixel art style. The reason there are so little pixels is because I used a 16 pixel by 16 pixel frame. The good thing about pixel art is that it allows you to create small sized files whilst still having your art look good. The image in the middle is my attempt at creating a wallpaper with vector art. The programme I used to create the image is called Inkscape which is a vector art programme. The pros with vector art is that it allows you to infinitely scale up or scale down your image to any amount of pixels so it always looks smooth on any screen. The last image is a picture of me and my friend photoshopped with GIMP into the video game Super Smash Brothers Ultimate. I did this by using a chroma key (something that removes all of one color) to remove the background (a green screen). I then put the cropped out images of us into another image.
