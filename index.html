About Aaron

Ever since I was seven I was into tech. At first it was computer programming, which I had a real passion for. Later on in my primary school years I started using a programme called Scratch — a block-based coding language for kids to make animations, games, and simulators. This later sparked my joy of game development.

Since then I have branched out my skills more when it comes to tech and now know video editing, 3D modeling, and animation. On this website is the work I have created for my Year 10 Digitech class.
